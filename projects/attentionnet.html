A CNN-Transformer hybrid model for encrypted network traffic classification, paired with a real-time analysis dashboard.

OVERVIEW

Attention Net classifies encrypted network traffic without decryption by analysing packet-level features. The CNN layers extract local byte-pattern features while the Transformer's self-attention mechanism captures long-range dependencies across packet sequences.
